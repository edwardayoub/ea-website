Post-doctoral F32 Fellowship — NIH/NCI (2022–2024)

Kirschstein National Research Service Award (NRSA) for Individual Postdoctoral Fellows (F32) for my project entitled: "Targeting Quiescence Pathways to Overcome Chemoresistance in EVI1-Overexpressing Leukemia". (link)

The University of Texas MD Anderson acknowledged this achievement in the departmental news.

Kimberly Patterson Fellowship in Leukemia — June 2020

The Kimberly Patterson Fellowship in Leukemia Research at The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center honors the memory of a courageous patient and her wish to support research to eradicate the disease. The fellowship is offered by MDACC as a recognition for research contributions and potential for excellence.

TRIUMPH Postdoctoral Fellowship (2019–2022)

The MD Anderson Cancer Prevention Research Institute of Texas (CPRIT) granted a TRIUMPH (Translational Research in Multi-Disciplinary Program) Fellowship for my postdoctoral training in cancer translational and clinical research.

Pre-doctoral F31 Fellowship — NIH/NHLBI (2018–2019)

National Institutes of Health (NIH) / National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI) granted a Ruth L. Kirschstein Predoctoral Individual National Research Service Award (NRSA) (Parent F31) for my project entitled: "Therapeutic Strategies for Anemia in 3q26 Rearranged Leukemia". (link)

The University of Rochester Medical Center acknowledged this achievement in the departmental news.
